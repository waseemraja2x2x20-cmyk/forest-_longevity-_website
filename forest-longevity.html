Philosophy / Three Pillars

live longer.

This isn’t a workout program. It’s a living system — how we move, breathe, eat and rest inside a forest cabin built from shipping containers. Minimal gear forces mastery.

Coach note

01

Movement — Functional over heavy

We don’t chase PRs. We chase daily capability: sit to stand without hands, carry firewood, hike without soreness. Two kettlebells and your body weight build durable strength, not show muscle.

Strength that transfers to life.

02

Nature — Forest air, morning light, wood

Wood floors give feedback. Morning light sets your clock. Cold air + hot water resets your nervous system. The cabin isn’t a backdrop — it’s equipment.

Nervous system reset by design.

03

Minimalism — One bell, one strap, your body

Constraints create consistency. If it doesn’t fit in a backpack, we don’t use it. Every move here was filmed on the same deck with one camera, one light — proof you don’t need a gym.

If you can’t do it here, you won’t do it anywhere.

The 5-Move Protocol + Bonus

Six patterns.
One forest deck.

01

Goblet Squat

FOUNDATIONAL STRENGTH • 12 REPS × 4 SETS

Builds daily life strength, protects knees, restores hip mobility lost to chairs. The single best predictor of staying independent after 70.

02

TRX Row

POSTURE & PULL • 12 REPS × 4 SETS

Counters sitting, retains back muscle for bone health and posture. Pull strength = longevity strength.

03

Hamstring Stretch Deck

MOBILITY • 30S HOLD × 3 / LEG

Improves flexibility, prevents falls, unlocks walking power. Tight hamstrings age your gait faster than anything.

04

Kettlebell Swing

POWER & VITALITY • 20 REPS × 4 SETS

Cardio + power without joint pounding. Trains hip snap for vitality, metabolic health, and fall resilience.

05

Dual Kettlebell Farmer Carry

GRIP & BALANCE • 40M × 3 CARRIES

Best longevity predictor — grip strength correlates directly with lifespan. Enhances stability on uneven forest trails.

06

Battle Rope Waves

BONUS • CONDITIONING • 20S ON / 40S OFF × 5

Finisher for heart and lungs. Low joint stress, high output — the forest air does the rest.

Featured: Hamstring Stretch Deck

Mobility • Tempo: breathe 4-6 / soft pulse

Why for longevity: Improves flexibility, prevents falls, unlocks walking power.

Nature cue: “Morning light on the deck. Let cold air soften the stretch, not force it.”

How to: Foot on bench, hinge at hips with flat back until gentle pull. No bouncing. Nose toward knee, breathe long. Use deck rail for balance.

Common mistake: Rounding low back to reach further. Hinge, don’t crunch.

Nutrition — The Container Kitchen

Eat like you live
in a juice bar.

The cabin kitchen is a single container — open, bright, no hiding. Everything you see is fuel. After forest training, you don’t need shakes. You need water, color, and chew.

Whole fruit only • No powders

Green juice

Cucumber, kale, lime, ginger

Mixed fruit bowl

Berries, orange, pineapple — anti-inflammatory

Nuts & seeds

Almonds + pumpkin — magnesium for sleep
  1. Hydration first. 500ml water + pinch salt + lemon before any food. Forest air is dry — you lose more than you think.
  2. Whole fruit, not juice alone. Blender, not extractor. Fiber slows sugar, feeds gut. Green juice + whole fruit bowl = complete.
  3. Anti-inflammatory by default. Berries, leafy greens, nuts, olive oil. No seed oils in the container. Simple fuel after simple work.

Post-workout: green juice (hydration + micronutrients), mixed fruit bowl (glycogen), handful nuts (satiety). Eat on the deck, barefoot, sunlight on skin.

Recovery — Forest Reset

Hot steam.
Cold air.
Sleep deep.

Recovery is not a luxury — it’s the longevity work. The cabin’s hot tub is 102°F. The forest air at night is 38°F. That contrast is medicine.

01

Contrast therapy

20 min hot tub + 2 min standing in cold forest air. 2 rounds. Vagus nerve reset. You’ll sleep like you hiked all day.
02

Sunlight anchor

Morning: 10 min on deck, no sunglasses. Evening: watch cabin lights from tub. Light tells your body when to repair.
03

Wood floor sleep

Cabin is quiet, dark, cool. No screens after tub. 8 hours minimum. The wood creaks — you don’t.

19:42 • Forest cabin • 38°F outside — Contrast = longevity: Hot 20’ • Cold 2’ • Repeat
